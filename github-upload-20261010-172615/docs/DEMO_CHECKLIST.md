# Demo recording checklist

Before recording, start the app, check the OpenAI configuration, and keep all credential files and unrelated windows out of the recording. Use a public research PDF you can inspect yourself.

1. Explain the purpose and stack briefly: browser interface, FastAPI backend, SQLite storage, arXiv, and OpenAI.
2. Search a topic. Show a result's title, authors, year, abstract, and paper link.
3. Save the result, open My library, and refresh to demonstrate persistence.
4. Upload a different PDF from your computer. Show extracted metadata, correct an imperfect field, inspect a body page, and open the original PDF.
5. Generate a summary. Point to information from the methods/results, then expand its supporting passages.
6. Ask a question about the datasets or baselines. Show the answer and matching page evidence.
7. Ask an out-of-scope question to demonstrate acknowledgement of missing information.
8. Refresh or restart, reopen the paper, and show that the AI history persists.

Mention the actual limits: text-based PDFs, imperfect metadata extraction, external service availability, and AI answers requiring review. Do not claim that these checks guarantee correct scientific interpretation.

The assignment requests a short video but does not specify an exact duration. Record your own narration and choose a length that demonstrates each required feature clearly.
